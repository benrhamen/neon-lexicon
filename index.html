How regular verbs work

Add -d or -ed: love → loved, work → worked. Use -d when the verb already ends in e. After a consonant + y, change y to i: study → studied. Some verbs double the last consonant: stop → stopped. The past participle is the same as the past simple for regular verbs.

For he / she / it, present simple normally adds -s or -es: works, watches. Some verbs change spelling: carries. Be uses am / is / are, have uses has, and do uses does.

Read keeps its spelling but changes sound: present rhymes with "seed"; past and participle rhyme with "red". Lie meaning "recline" is lie / lay / had lain; lie meaning "tell an untruth" is lie / lied / had lied.
